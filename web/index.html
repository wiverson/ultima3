Controls

Controller mode

D-pad
Move, or move the cursor in a menu (keyboard: WASD or arrows)
A
Open the command menu, choose (keyboard: Enter or Z)
B
Cancel, or pass a turn (keyboard: Escape or X)
X
Ztats (keyboard: C)
Y
Look, or Attack in combat (keyboard: V)

Pressing a gamepad button switches to controller mode. The command menu lists the commands your surroundings call for first. In either mode, walking into a townsperson talks to them, into a shop counter opens the shop, and into a locked door unlocks it.

Combat

Arrows
Move the active member
A
Attack in a direction (space for none); walking into a monster attacks it too
C N R Z V
Cast, Negate, Ready, Ztats, Volume
Escape
Turn auto combat off for this fight (B on a controller)

"Classic moves" keeps the Apple II rule: monsters may move and attack diagonally, the party may not. Untick it for the Macintosh version's diagonals. With "Auto combat" ticked above, the party fights by itself: it casts Repond or Pontori, heals the weakest, fires when lined up and closes to melee, as in the Macintosh version.

Prompts that ask "whom" take a member number 1-4; a direction is an arrow key. Your game is saved in this browser when you press Q on the surface and resumes on the next visit. URL options: ?new starts a fresh game, ?tiles=PC EGA (or another set: Apple II Color, Commodore 64, Nintendo, PC VGA, ...) changes the graphics, ?music=0 silences the music.
